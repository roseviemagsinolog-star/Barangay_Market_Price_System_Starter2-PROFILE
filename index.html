About the System

An overview of the system, its goal, and the principles guiding its use.

System Overview

The Barangay Market Price Information System is a simple web-based demo for viewing and comparing local crop prices. It helps community members find sample market listings by crop, category, and barangay.

System Goal

To make local crop-price information easier to access so farmers and community members can use it when planning sales and market visits. Before making decisions, users should confirm prices with local market officials because demo listings may not reflect current prices.

Ethical Standards

Principles for presenting and using market-price information responsibly.

Accuracy and Transparency

Clearly identify sample or unverified prices, include update dates, and avoid presenting demo information as an official market quote.

Privacy

Do not publish personal information without permission. The demo stores price entries in the current browser and does not provide a shared public database.

Fairness and Responsible Use

Present information neutrally and encourage users to verify prices with local sources rather than relying on a single listing for important decisions.
